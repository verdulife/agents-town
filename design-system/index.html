the rules

  1. Shadow is cold, light is warm. Structural shadow leans blue-violet; every practical light leans amber. That split is what makes the street read as dusk, not the detail.
  2. Nothing is pure. White is bone, ink is blue-black. No #fff and no #000 anywhere.
  3. Mid-tones stay desaturated: the saturation budget belongs to the lights, so walls, asphalt and sky stay quiet and the signs do not.
  4. Ramps step in hue as well as value — a wall's highlight is warmer than its base, its shadow cooler. Stepping brightness alone is what makes pixel art look flat.
